All About Sloths

Introduction to Sloths

Sloths are slow-moving mammals known for their leisurely pace and tree-dwelling lifestyle. They are native to Central and South American forests.

Habitat of Sloths

Inhabit tropical forests, sloths are perfectly adapted to live in the trees where they find both food and shelter.

Behavior of Sloths

Sloths are solitary animals known for their low-energy lifestyle. They sleep 15-20 hours a day, and are mostly active during the night.

Conservation efforts for Sloths

While some sloth species are of least concern, others face habitat loss and require conservation efforts to protect them.
