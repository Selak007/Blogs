All About Dogs

Dogs are known for their loyalty and companionship. They have been close partners of humans for centuries.

Characteristics

  • Diverse breeds with various sizes
  • Strong sense of smell
  • Social animals

Likes

  • Enjoys going for walks
  • Loves to play fetch

Fun Fact

Dogs have three eyelids. The third lid, called a nictitating membrane or "haw," helps keep the eye moist and protected.
