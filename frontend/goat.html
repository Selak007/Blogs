Introduction to Goats

Goats are one of the oldest domesticated species of animal, and have been used for milk, meat, fur, and skins across much of the world.

Habitat of Goats

Goats are extremely adaptable and can live in a variety of environments from mountains to deserts.

Behavior of Goats

Goats are social animals and prefer to be in groups. They are also known for their curiosity and intelligence.

Conservation efforts for Goats

While domesticated goats are not at risk, certain wild goat species are threatened by habitat loss and hunting.
