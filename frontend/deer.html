All About Deer

Introduction

Deer are a fascinating group of animals known for their grace and beauty. They are found in various regions across the globe, each species adapting to its environment.

Habitat

Deer thrive in diverse habitats including forests, grasslands, and even mountainous regions.

Diet

Most deer are herbivores, feeding on leaves, shoots, and other plant-based materials.

Behavior

Deer are known for their agility and speed. They are often social animals, living in groups known as herds.
