# Lion

## Introduction
Lions are majestic big cats known as the "king of the jungle". They are iconic symbols of strength and courage, and they primarily inhabit the grasslands and savannas of Africa.

## Characteristics
Lions are social animals that live in groups called prides, consisting of females, their young, and a few males. Male lions are known for their impressive manes, which play a role in attracting mates.

## Social Structure
The pride's structure is primarily matriarchal, with females collaborating for hunting and raising the cubs. The males, while often at the periphery, protect the pride’s territory.

## Habitat Details
Lions prefer open plains and woodlands, allowing them to support their hunting which relies heavily on stealth and the element of surprise.

## Conservation Status
Lions are currently classified as a vulnerable species due to habitat loss, human-wildlife conflict, and illegal poaching. Conservation efforts focus on ecological balance and habitat preservation.

## Likes
Lions spend much of their time resting, with females conducting the majority of hunting mostly during the cooler times of day.

## Fun Fact
A lion’s roar can be heard up to 8 kilometers away, serving as a powerful tool for pride communication and territorial marking.
