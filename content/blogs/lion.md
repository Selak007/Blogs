# All About Lions

Lions are majestic creatures known as the "king of the jungle." They are powerful and sociable animals usually found in groups called prides.

## Characteristics
- Large size and strength
- Distinctive manes in males
- Roaring voices that can be heard up to 5 miles away

## Likes
- Prefers living in groups
- Loves to hunt in teamwork

## Fun Fact
Lions can sleep up to 20 hours a day. They mostly hunt at night, during the cooler times of the day.
