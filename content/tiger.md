# All About Tigers

## Introduction
Tigers are the largest wild cats in the world. They are known for their powerful bodies, colorful orange coats, and black stripes.

## Habitat
Tigers can be found in the forests and grasslands of Asia. They require large territories to hunt and raise their young.

## Diet
Tigers primarily feed on large mammals, such as deer and wild boar. They are apex predators in their environment.

## Behavior
Most tigers are solitary and territorial animals. They communicate through vocalizations and scent marks.

## Conservation Status
Tigers are endangered due to habitat loss, poaching, and human-wildlife conflict. Conservation efforts are crucial for their survival.

## Interesting Facts
- Tigers are excellent swimmers and enjoy bathing.
- Each tiger has a unique pattern of stripes, much like human fingerprints.
